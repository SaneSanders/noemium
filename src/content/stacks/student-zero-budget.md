## The recipe

1. **Get GitHub Copilot free through GitHub Education.** Verify your student
   status once and you get the full Copilot Pro feature set for free —
   completions, chat and basic PR help inside VS Code or JetBrains.
2. **Dump lecture notes and readings into NotebookLM.** It answers grounded
   questions over your uploaded PDFs and slides, and the audio overview is a
   genuine study hack for commutes. Free tier is generous.
3. **Use Google AI Studio for coding help and long documents.** The free
   Gemini API tier has huge context and decent quotas for homework-scale
   prompts. Keep an eye on the terms if you paste sensitive coursework.
4. **ChatGPT free tier for general questions.** Good enough for explanations,
   brainstorming and rewrites. Save the deep coding conversations for Copilot
   and AI Studio so you do not burn the daily cap on trivial asks.
5. **Organize everything in Notion with the free AI trial.** Notion AI is
   limited on the free/Plus plan, but the trial covers enough to summarize
   notes and draft outlines. Do not pay for Notion Business just for AI.
6. **Build a source habit.** Free tools hallucinate too. Cite the source,
   verify the claim, and never turn in an AI-generated reference list without
   checking it.

## What you pay

- **GitHub Copilot — free via GitHub Education, $0/mo.** The student program
  unlocks the paid Pro tier for verified students; without it, the free tier
  is too limited for real coursework.
- **NotebookLM — free tier, $0/mo.** Grounded reading and audio overviews are
  fully free for student-volume notebooks.
- **Google AI Studio — free tier, $0/mo.** The free Gemini API quota covers
  coding help and long-document questions at student scale.
- **ChatGPT — free tier, $0/mo.** General Q&A and brainstorming; the free
  daily cap is enough if you route coding to Copilot and AI Studio.
- **Notion AI — limited trial on free/Plus, $0/mo.** Use the trial credits for
  summaries and outlines; do not upgrade to Business unless your whole team
  already lives in Notion.

## When this breaks down

Free tiers have daily caps, context limits and no support. The week before
finals, when every tool throttles at once, is when you learn the real cost
of $0. Plan ahead: queue bulk tasks overnight and keep local backups of
anything you store in a free cloud tool.
